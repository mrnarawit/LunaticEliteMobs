# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - First release
- Hostile mobs can spawn as elites in 4 tiers (Elite, Empowered, Warlord, Diabolic) with more health, damage, speed, armor and XP.
- Tiered auras (particles, orbs, blades, sigil, wings) that scale with the mob's hitbox.
- Tier name prefixes with colors.
- Bonus loot: enchanted weapons, RPG Series gear ranked by stats, weighted treasure pool, rare extras.
- Filters by id, tag, mod and dimension; guaranteed elites; boss exclusion.
- Delayed elite roll for In Control and health-scaling mods.
- Commands `/lem summon|make|clear|info|reloadloot`.
